# Your walkthrough checklist — one full campaign, start to finish

**What you're doing:** building one real campaign on zapcampaigns.com, watching every piece get made, then sending
it to Meta (as a paused ad) and to GoHighLevel. If this goes cleanly, the product is ready to move forward.

**The setup (don't change these):**
- Your own account: **arfeen@arfeenkhan.com**
- Campaign type: **Lead Magnet**
- Path: **"I'll pick as we go"** (the manual one — not "Build it all for me")
- Meta ads: **Paused** (nothing spends money)

**Time needed:** about 1½ hours in one sitting. The GoHighLevel connection only lasts about a day, so connect it and
push in the same sitting.

⚠️ **Meta's login expires on 5 October 2026.** Do this walkthrough before then, or reconnect Meta first (step 3).

---

## Part 1 — Before you start (about 15 minutes)

☐ **1. Tell CC you're starting.** Nothing should be deployed while you're building — a deploy mid-build can leave a
step stuck.

☐ **2. Delete the leftover test ad in Meta.**
   - Open **Meta Ads Manager** and choose the ad account **KS 1**.
   - In **Campaigns**, search for **ZZ-GATE-POSITIVE-ARM**.
   - Tick it, then **Delete**. It is paused and has never spent anything; it's a leftover test.
   - Leave the ones named **"Auto Campaign Kit"** alone — they're a separate decision.
   - Afterwards, tell CC it's gone — ZAP still has a record of it that CC can tidy with your go-ahead.

☐ **3. Check Meta is connected.**
   - On zapcampaigns.com, open **Settings** (`zapcampaigns.com/v2-dashboard/settings`), section **Integrations**.
   - **Meta Ads** should say **Connected**, ad account **KS 1**.
   - If it says **Not connected**, click **Connect Meta Ads** and follow Facebook's screens.

☐ **4. Reconnect GoHighLevel.** On the same Settings page, under **GoHighLevel**:
   - It will most likely say **Not connected** (the old connection expired in July). Click **Connect GoHighLevel**.
   - Choose your location **The Incredible You Management** and approve.
   - Back in ZAP it should now say **Connected**, with that location name.

☐ **5. Check the snapshot status.** Right under GoHighLevel there is a coloured label:
   - 🟢 **"Snapshot active — 12/16 workflows"** (or any number of 12 or more) → **go to Part 2.**
   - 🟠 **"Incomplete install"** or 🔴 **"Snapshot not applied"** → do step 6 first.
   - Tip: the small link next to the label re-checks it.

☐ **6. Only if the label wasn't green — the snapshot fix (already approved).**
   - Tell CC: *"The snapshot status isn't green — set the snapshot setting."* Give CC the **ZAP Master Snapshot ID** from
     GoHighLevel (agency view → **Account Snapshots** → the ZAP Master Snapshot → the ID is in the page address).
   - Once it's set, the live site restarts by itself (about 2–3 minutes). Wait until CC says it's back up.
   - Refresh **Settings**. An orange button **Apply ZAP Master Snapshot →** now appears under GoHighLevel. Click it and
     finish the steps GoHighLevel shows you.
   - Come back and re-check the label. **Don't start Part 2 until it's green.**

---

## Part 2 — Build the campaign (about 1 hour)

☐ **7.** Go to the **Dashboard** and click **Start New Campaign**.

☐ **8.** Describe your real business in the box — a few full sentences about who you help and how. Click
**That's me** when it has understood you.

☐ **9.** Zappy builds your ideal customer profile. If it offers a few extra questions to sharpen it, answer them or
skip them — either is fine.

☐ **10.** When asked what kind of campaign, choose **Lead Magnet**.

☐ **11.** When asked how to build it, choose **I'll pick as we go**. (Not "Build it all for me".)

☐ **12.** If Zappy asks for a testimonial or any campaign details, answer them.

☐ **13. For each piece, in this order — Offer, Method, Lead Magnet, Headlines, Ad Copy, Landing Page:**
   - Click **Show me options**.
   - Look at the cards and pick the one you like.
   - Click **Lock it in →**.

   🛑 **Never press "Skip — I already have this."** It quietly stops the campaign from ever being marked complete,
   and then the Push button won't work — even though the chat still says everything is done.
   (If Zappy asks for an event date or time for a free next step, you *can* skip that — that's a different question.)

☐ **14. At Landing Page, look at the progress bar at the top.** The **Landing Page** circle must fill in.
   - If it stays empty while the chat says it's done, **stop** and send CC the page address (it has the campaign
     number in it). The page didn't publish, and Meta can't push without it.

☐ **15.** Email, WhatsApp and Ad Images each appear one at a time. For Ad Images, pick a style when asked.

☐ **16.** At the end, click **Open my Campaign Kit**.

---

## Part 3 — Check it and push it (about 20 minutes)

☐ **17. Read it through.** On the Campaign Kit page, check the pieces tell **one story**: the same audience, the same
promise, and the same lead magnet name across the offer, headline, ad copy, landing page and emails. Note anything
that doesn't match.

☐ **18. Push to Meta.** Click **Push to Meta / GHL**. In the Meta section:
   - Leave the status on **Paused**. **Do not choose "Active"** — that starts spending immediately.
   - Click **Push to Meta**.
   - If it's blocked, it tells you why — take a screenshot of the message and send it to CC.

☐ **19. Push to GoHighLevel.** In the same window, click **Push to GHL**.
   - Use the separate **Push to GHL** button, not "Push to both".

☐ **20. Check GoHighLevel itself — don't trust the green message.** ZAP can show success even when nothing
arrived.
   - In GoHighLevel, open the location **The Incredible You Management** → **Settings** → **Custom Values**.
   - Search **ZAP**. You should see entries such as **ZAP Email 1 Subject**, **ZAP Headlines**, **ZAP Landing Page**,
     **ZAP Lead Magnet URL** — **containing this campaign's words**.
   - If they're missing, empty, or show an old campaign's text, the push didn't work. Screenshot it for CC.

☐ **21. Check Meta itself.** In Meta Ads Manager, find the new campaign (it has the name you gave it):
   - The campaign, ad set and ad all say **Paused**.
   - The ad shows the right image, the right text and a link to your landing page.

☐ **22. Send CC:**
   - the Campaign Kit page address;
   - a screenshot of the ZAP Custom Values in GoHighLevel;
   - a screenshot of the paused ad in Meta;
   - anything that looked wrong or didn't match in step 17.

---

**If anything errors or gets stuck at any point:** take a screenshot, note which step you were on, and stop there.
Don't retry the same step more than twice.
