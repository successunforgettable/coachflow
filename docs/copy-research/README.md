## 2. 🔴 SAME UNVERIFIED-PAYLOAD DISCIPLINE AS `docs/lead-magnet-research/`

Every statistic, citation bracket and named study in these reports is **unverified**. The bracketed
numbers point at NotebookLM's own source set, not at anything audited here.

**No statistic from this folder may reach a prompt, a rule file a prompt interpolates, a UI string,
or generated copy of any kind.** A fabricated number that arrives through a *prompt* is laundered
past the compliance axis, the fabrication validator and the persistence gate.

**Additionally, and specific to this set: the neuroscience is decorative.** Three of the seven
reports lean on an amygdala/prefrontal-cortex account of why hard-sell copy fails, and treat it as
load-bearing explanation. **Keep the observation (hard-sell language raises reader defences);
discard the mechanism-claim.** Do not write "the amygdala flags this as a threat" into any prompt,
comment, or standard as a justification. Same rule as the statistics: **keep the framework, discard
the payload.**

## 3. Where these reports are about a PERSON and we are prompting a MODEL

The whole set describes **human beings writing under commercial pressure**. Its causal model is
psychological — imposter syndrome, deadline stress, schoolroom conditioning, fear of sounding
amateurish. **A generator has none of that.**

Several prescriptions therefore transfer with the right conclusion attached to a reason that is
false for our case, which is exactly the shape CLAUDE.md §15i warns about. **Adopt the mechanic;
re-derive the reason for a model.** Do not bank the psychological explanation as ours.

The read-out of which parts transfer, which invert, and which do not transfer at all is in
`CHECKPOINT.md` under the copy-craft entry — written cold, before comparison with the strategy
side's own read.
