## 0. Open Graph tags — the part Meta actually reads

*"The crawler focuses on Open Graph (OG) tags (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url) to interpret page content… the crawler adheres to the 'first 1MB' rule… It is critical that OG tags and safety-related metadata are placed high within the section."* — Compliance and Policy Scanning Systems, §2

**og:title:** Free live workshop: building something beyond the salary

**og:description:** A Sunday session for professionals in the UAE and Mauritius who've saved carefully and want to understand what to do next. No experience needed. Free to attend.

**og:image:** 1200 × 630, absolute reachable URL. Any text baked into it is read by OCR, so it must carry the same problem-unaware framing as the ad — no crypto vocabulary.

**og:url:** the canonical page URL.

Three build requirements that go with them:

- Server-rendered. If JavaScript injects these tags, the crawler may never see them.
- At the very top of `<head>`, above stylesheets, fonts, analytics and any inline script or base64 block.
- Deliberately no crypto vocabulary, matching the problem-unaware ads that point here. *"If OG tags are missing, the system attempts to generate information automatically, which frequently results in unprofessional previews and manual review triggers."*

## Build notes — now policy requirements, not polish

The research reclassifies four of these. *"Low-Quality Content: Pages with broken elements, placeholder text, or excessive ad-to-content ratios"* is a named violation category, and the consequence spreads: *"Andromeda clusters visually similar ads under a single Entity ID. If a landing page associated with an Entity ID is flagged for Disruptive UX, all ads sharing that visual 'ticket' will face retrieval suppression."* One broken page can throttle a whole cluster of ads.

**1. Dead countdown.** Currently reads 00:00:00:00 with "Doors open on —" beneath it. Broken element plus placeholder text — both named. Wire it or remove it.

**2. Duplicated curriculum steps.** STEP 01 and STEP 03 each appear twice in the live build.

**3. Empty testimonial section.** "What the Community Says" has a heading, an intro, and nothing under it.

**4. Stacked fake scarcity.** "92% spots claimed", "Only 5 Left", "Spot Expires in 8:00 minutes" together on one page. *"Misleading Claims: False attributes, non-functional features, and deceptive value propositions that do not align with the ad creative."* Keep one honest constraint — a real seat cap — and drop the rest.

**5. Currency drift.** AED 2,499, AED 997, AED 4,997 and INR 2,447 all appear on the live pages. Any price mismatch between ad and page triggers the Friction Penalty. Pick one.

**6. Replay contradiction.** The live pages give different answers on whether a replay exists. Pick one and use it across all three, and make it match the ads.

**7. Celebrity quotes next to the offer.** Robbins, Bachchan and Roshan praising Arfeen personally, placed directly above a crypto offer, reads as though they're endorsing the crypto. Move that block above "Who leads it."

**8. Compliance.** "Build Wealth Beyond Your Salary" and "Learn How To Make Your Hard-Earned Money Work Smarter" both imply a financial outcome. The UAE regulates financial promotions on top of Meta's rules. This copy promises understanding and a process, never a result — keep it that way through any edits.

## Technical requirements — the developer's half

The copy governs what a human reads. This governs whether Meta can deliver the ad at all. *"While landing pages are critical for ad quality scoring and conversion signal hygiene, they do not function as a primary retrieval signal for audience matching."* — Semantic Retrieval, §7. The page earns its delivery value through the conversion signal, which is why this section matters more than any headline.

**Pixel and CAPI, both.** *"Relying on the Meta Pixel alone results in a 30% loss of signal, which blinds the Andromeda retrieval engine."* Both must send the same event with the same `event_id` for deduplication.

**One conversion event**, firing on successful registration — not on page view, not on button click. Lead or CompleteRegistration, the same one across all three pages.

**Event parameters, and the hashing rule that silently breaks everything:**

| parameter | hashing |
|---|---|
| fbc (click ID, from `fbclid`) | **never hash** |
| fbp (browser cookie) | **never hash** |
| email, phone, first/last name | SHA-256, lowercased and trimmed |
| IP address, user agent | unhashed |

*"fbc (Click ID): Must be passed without hashing; hashing breaks matching."* Capture `fbclid` on arrival and persist it, or fbc goes missing for anyone who takes more than one page view to register. Target EMQ 7.0+.

**Crawler access.** *"If a website is slow to load or blocks the facebookexternalhit robot, it creates a 'Signal Vacuum.' This blindfolds Andromeda."* Allow `facebookexternalhit` explicitly through Cloudflare, WAF, bot protection and rate limiting. Serve meaningful HTML on first response. Run the Sharing Debugger and click "Scrape Again" after every content change — Meta caches aggressively.

**Learning phase arithmetic, for whoever runs the ads.** *"A minimum of 50 conversion events per 7-day window is required to exit the learning phase."* Daily budget = (target CPA × 50) ÷ 7.
