# The Digital Asset Blueprint — PAGE THREE: WOMEN

*Page three of three. Written against the Andromeda landing-page research in `docs/andromeda/landing-page-research/`.*

**The reader:** woman, 35–55, UAE and Mauritius. Some in professional roles, some running households. Usually the person who actually tracks the family's money. Wants something that is hers, and something that lasts for her children. Has been talked past by every crypto conversation she has encountered. Has never bought crypto.

**Governing principle:** she is not joining for crypto. She's joining because she wants something of her own and nobody has ever explained this to her properly. Crypto is the vehicle and it arrives late. Nothing on this page promises a return, a number, or a timeframe.

**A note on the existing page.** The current headline is "Build wealth while being a housewife" — it defines her by what she isn't doing. Everything below names what she already does well and asks the question she's actually asking. The failure mode for this audience is condescension, and the guard against it is simple: never explain her own life to her, and never imply she needs simplifying-down. She needs the same content as everyone else, told to her rather than around her.

**No pain on this page appears on pages one or two.** Distinct pains produce distinct Persona-Desire-Awareness angles, and distinct angles earn separate retrieval tickets rather than collapsing into one Entity ID.

## Two notes specific to this page

**Tone.** The failure mode here is condescension, and it usually arrives disguised as kindness — simplified content, softer promises, gentler language, fewer numbers. Don't. She gets the same session and the same rigour as everyone else. What changes is that nothing is assumed, not that anything is watered down. If a line would read as patronising when said aloud to a woman who runs a household budget better than most people run a business, cut it.
