## 3. What's stopping you

*Pains: judgement that doesn't transfer · needing control · the pitch reflex*

**Eyebrow:** THE REAL REASON

**Heading:** It was never that you didn't know enough.

**Intro:** After twenty-five years working with people on exactly this, I can tell you what actually stops them. It isn't information.

**Your judgement doesn't travel.**
Inside your industry you can read a deal in ten minutes and you're usually right. Outside it you have no way to tell a sensible move from a reckless one — so you defer, and deferring feels like discipline rather than avoidance.

**You've never handed money to something you don't run.**
Every dirham you've made came from something you controlled directly. Putting capital somewhere you can't personally influence the outcome contradicts how you've operated your entire working life.

**Everyone is selling you something.**
You get pitched weekly. You've built a reflex for it, and that reflex — correct almost every time — is also what's kept you out of the one thing that might have been worth looking at.

**Closing:** Most people fail at this because of how they behave, not because of what they know. That's where Sunday starts.

## Build notes and technical requirements

Identical to page one — see `page-1-professionals.md` for the full detail. In summary:

**Policy requirements, not polish.** Broken elements and placeholder text are a named low-quality violation, and a page flagged for disruptive UX suppresses every ad sharing that Entity ID. So: no dead countdown, no unfilled tokens, no duplicated sections, no empty testimonial band, one honest scarcity constraint rather than three invented ones, one currency, and a replay answer that matches the ads.

**Signal requirements.** Pixel and CAPI both, deduplicated by `event_id`. One conversion event on successful registration. fbc and fbp unhashed; email, phone and name SHA-256 hashed; IP and user agent unhashed. `fbclid` captured on arrival and persisted. Target EMQ 7.0+. `facebookexternalhit` allowed through all bot protection, and Sharing Debugger re-scraped after every content change.

**Compliance.** Nothing on this page promises a return, a number, a timeframe or a guarantee. The UAE regulates financial promotions on top of Meta's own rules — keep it that way through any edits.
