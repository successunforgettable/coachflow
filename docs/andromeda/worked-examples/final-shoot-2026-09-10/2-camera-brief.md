# Camera Brief — Nine Ad Videos
## Digital Asset Blueprint

Nine videos, one session. One person on camera. **Three locations, three lighting setups.**

---

# Why the setups must change

Meta groups near-identical videos into one entity and treats them as a single ad. It judges similarity on the picture, not the words.

**These separate nothing:** a different shirt, a different chair, a slight zoom.
**These do:** whether the face is in frame, what kind of space it is, the lighting character.

Nine videos shot in one room from one position gets you the reach of one.

---

# The nine setups

| # | Face | Space | Lighting | In frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Full Face | Professional / desk | High-key | Straight to camera |
| **P2** | Hands Only | Residential | Low-key | Your hands, a phone showing a banking app, a coffee going cold beside it. |
| **P3** | Writing | OUTDOORS | Natural | Outdoors, a notebook on a table. You writing a short numbered list as you speak. |
| **E1** | Hands Only | Professional / desk | Natural | Your hands on a laptop, then a stack of invoices, then the laptop closing. |
| **E2** | Writing | Residential | High-key | You drawing two boxes on paper, one large then one small beside it. |
| **E3** | Full Face | OUTDOORS — wide shot | Low-key | Wide shot, considerable space around you. Not a desk. |
| **W1** | Writing | Professional / desk | Low-key | You writing four short unlabelled lines on a board. |
| **W2** | Full Face | Residential | Natural | Straight to camera |
| **W3** | Hands Only | OUTDOORS | High-key | Outdoors. Hands turning the pages of a notebook, then closing it. |

**Three of the nine have no face in any frame** — P2, E1, W3. Not a mistake. Do not cut in a reaction shot.

**Lighting definitions:** High-key is bright and flat with minimal shadow. Natural is warm daylight from a window or open shade. Low-key is directional with genuinely deep shadows.

---

# 🔴 THE ONE THING THAT CANNOT BE FIXED LATER

**P3, E3 and W3 are outdoors. Each needs a dark or plain low-detail area in frame for a text card to sit on in the edit.**

Bokeh, deep shadow, a plain wall, a flat surface. Anything that is not bright sky or busy sunlit background.

Every video gets a small text line burned in afterwards. Meta reads it with OCR to work out what the ad is about. White text over a bright background cannot be read, the ad loses its category signal, **and nothing anywhere reports the failure.**

W3 especially — bright outdoor shot with hands in frame. Find the shadow.

---

# Format

**Shoot 9:16 vertical**, subject centred so it crops to 4:5 and 1:1 without losing anything.

**Leave clearance top and bottom** for platform interface and the text card.

**No music, no cuts, no camera moves.** One continuous take each. These should look like a person talking, not a production.

Each script runs about 42 seconds. Record several takes and let him settle.

---

# Shooting order

1. **Professional / desk block** — P1 (full face, high-key), E1 (hands only, natural), W1 (writing, low-key)
2. **Residential block** — P2 (hands, low-key), E2 (drawing, high-key), W2 (full face, natural)
3. **Outdoors** — P3 (writing, natural), W3 (hands, bright — find the shadow), E3 (full face wide, late light)

Block 3 depends on daylight. E3 wants late light with real shadows, so plan the outdoor block for the end of the afternoon and shoot E3 last.
