# Editor Brief — Nine Ad Videos
## Digital Asset Blueprint

Nine videos, about 42 seconds each. Minimal edit. The one piece of real work is a text card on each.

---

# Treatment

**No music. No cuts. No graphics, transitions or animation beyond the single text card below.**

These are cold-audience ads. Polish makes a viewer who has never heard of the person more suspicious, not less. One continuous take.

**Captions on**, burned in, not platform auto-captions. Most of this is watched with sound off.

---

# 🔴 The text card

Each video gets one short line **burned into the exported file.** Not an overlay added in Ads Manager. Meta reads it with OCR and can only read what is in the video itself.

| # | Text |
|---|---|
| P1 | `DIGITAL ASSETS · FROM ZERO · SUNDAY` |
| P2 | `CRYPTO · EXPLAINED · FREE SUNDAY` |
| P3 | `CRYPTO · A FIXED SEQUENCE · LIVE` |
| E1 | `DIGITAL ASSETS · THE MECHANICS` |
| E2 | `CRYPTO FOR BUSINESS OWNERS · FREE` |
| E3 | `CRYPTO DUE DILIGENCE · SUNDAY LIVE` |
| W1 | `DIGITAL ASSETS · FOR WOMEN · FREE` |
| W2 | `CRYPTO · WHAT IT EVEN IS · SUNDAY` |
| W3 | `CRYPTO IN PLAIN WORDS · SUNDAY` |

Do not reword, abbreviate or tidy these. The exact words are what gets read.

**Inside the first three seconds.** Meta scores the opening three seconds separately.
**Sans-serif. High contrast.** Plain and legible beats designed.
**Modest, not dominant.** A small lower-third for a few seconds. A text-heavy video gets classified as a utility ad rather than an emotional one.
**Inside platform safe zones** so it is not covered by interface on Reels and Stories.
**On P3, E3 and W3 — the outdoor shots — place the card over the dark or plain area the camera team framed for it.**

# Export

**9:16 vertical** master, subject centred so 4:5 and 1:1 crops lose nothing.

H.264, MP4. Nothing exotic.

**Name each file by its code** — P1, P2, P3, E1, E2, E3, W1, W2, W3. Each pairs with its own headline and text card. If they get mixed up, the wrong text goes with the wrong video.

---

# Do not

- Add music, however quiet
- Cut between takes mid-script
- Add a logo, endcard or animated intro
- Colour grade heavily — the lighting differences between videos are deliberate and must survive
- Add a face to P2, E1 or W3. Those are hands-only by design
