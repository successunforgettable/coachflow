# Your Scripts — Nine Ad Videos
## The person on camera

Nine videos, one session. You speak in all nine. You are **fully visible in three**, **partly visible in three**, and **not seen at all in three** — hands and voice only.

That split is deliberate. Meta groups near-identical videos of the same person and shows them as if they were one ad. Changing shirt or chair does not separate them. Changing whether your face is in frame does.

---

# What changed from the last version

The four patterns are now named in the **first ten seconds** of every script, instead of near the end.

Controlled A/B audits at over 100,000 impressions per variant found that ads naming their mechanism early get about half the watch time and roughly **double the click-through, three times the landing page conversion, and half the cost per acquisition.** Naming it late looks better on watch time and performs worse on everything that matters.

That evidence is rated Tier 2 — the effect is measured, but landing page and offer quality could not be fully isolated. The trade is real in both directions: you lose 10 to 14 points of hook rate and 23 to 30 points of hold rate. The ads will look worse on watch time. That is expected.

---

# How to deliver these

**Read each one aloud before you shoot it.** If you stumble, the line is written rather than spoken. Change it there and then.

**Do not rush.** Each is about 42 seconds at a natural pace. If you finish in 35 you have gone too fast.

**One thought, one breath.** No sentence here is longer than 18 words. If you run out of air, break it.

**Do not narrate what is on screen.** In the writing and hands videos the camera already shows what you're doing.

**No selling and no credentials.** Nothing about returns, wealth, income or getting rich appears anywhere. No Tony Robbins, no Fortune 500, no twelve books. Shez's seven years is the only credential used, and only in three of the nine.

**Wear something different per block** so two ads don't look like the same take.
