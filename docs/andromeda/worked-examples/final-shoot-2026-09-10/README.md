## What this version did — the front-loaded mechanism

The four patterns moved from roughly **second 28** into the **first ten seconds** of all nine
scripts, per `../../script-rule-spec.md` **§4.5**. Verified by recomputation at 2.7 words/second,
where twelve seconds is word 32: the mechanism lands at **3.7s to 8.9s** across the set.

⚠️ **§4.5 is an UNRESOLVED CONFLICT, not a settled rule.** The ZAP corpus puts the mechanism at
beat 3; `../../performance-research/` puts it at beat 2 and rates that **Tier 2**. These nine were
built on the Tier 2 side deliberately. **The nine creator scripts were left on the other side** so
the two can be compared once there are numbers.

**Verified at banking, recomputed not accepted:** 112–114 words across all nine · hooks 4–10 words ·
longest sentence 18 · zero six-word sequences repeated across more than two scripts · compliance
clean across scripts, cards and headlines · **all nine card-and-headline pairs share zero tokens,
every headline ≤27 characters, every card inside 30–50**.

## 🔴 The one instruction that cannot be fixed after the shoot

**P3, E3 and W3 are outdoors. Each needs a dark or plain low-detail region in frame for the text
card.** White text over a bright background cannot be read by OCR; the ad silently loses its
category anchor; **nothing reports the failure**. W3 is the hardest — bright outdoor, hands in frame.

Stated in both `2-camera-brief.md` and `4-production-brief.md`, with the reason attached in each.
