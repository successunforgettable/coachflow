### Creative Production Playbook: Meta Andromeda 2026 Prospecting Framework

#### 1\. THE MULTIMODAL SIGNALING ARCHITECTURE UNDER ANDROMEDA

##### Strategic Context: From Ranking-First to Retrieval-First

The transition to the Andromeda engine represents the most fundamental shift in Meta’s delivery logic in a decade. We have moved from a legacy "ranking-first" system to a "retrieval-first" architecture Affectgroup. In the old environment, ads entered the auction based on manual targeting and bids; today, Andromeda acts as a "bouncer" at the door. Creative is now the primary lever for targeting because the system retrieves ads based on pixel-level signals before they ever reach the auction stage. If Andromeda’s retrieval encoders do not categorize your asset as a match for a user's real-time intent, the ad is effectively pruned from the system and never reaches the auction, regardless of budget aggression Affectgroup.

##### Technical Deconstruction: Hardware, Encoders, and Entity IDs

This 10,000x increase in model complexity is powered by the  **NVIDIA Grace Hopper Superchip**  and  **MTIA silicon** , which eliminated the memory bandwidth bottlenecks that restricted previous iterations Affectgroup. Andromeda utilizes a multimodal stack—incorporating computer vision, Rosetta (OCR), and speech encoders—to analyze raw pixels, text, and audio. This generates an  **Entity ID** : a unique digital fingerprint assigned to the  *meaning*  and  *intent*  of an ad rather than the file itself Affectgroup.Growth Architects must avoid "Entity ID clustering," where multiple assets communicate the same idea. If ten ads share a bedroom setting and a "better sleep" hook, they receive the same Entity ID. These ads then cannibalize each other, fighting for the same auction slot. Furthermore, Andromeda organizes ads into a  **Hierarchical Index**  (a tree structure). If a portfolio lacks visual diversity, and the system "prunes" the specific branch your ads occupy (e.g.,  *Apparel \> Lifestyle* ), your entire campaign delivery will stall Affectgroup.

##### The DNA of Intent Branches

To navigate this tree structure, we utilize three distinct "Visual DNA profiles" to trigger different branches of the retrieval index:

* **Grounded:**  Focuses on utility, engineering, and data-heavy visualizations to navigate functional/analytical branches.  
* **Esoteric:**  Utilizes pattern-interrupts and abstract sequences to engage curiosity-driven or psychological branches.  
* **Aspirational:**  Leverages high-production lifestyle cues and emotional relief to capture lifestyle-centric intent Affectgroup.

#### 4\. POST-CLICK SIGNAL CONTINUITY & COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE

##### The Friction Penalty and Model Elasticity

Andromeda applies a "Friction Penalty" when the Visual DNA of an ad does not align with its destination Affectgroup. Because the engine uses  **Model Elasticity** —switching to a more complex version of the model for high-value users—a mismatch between the ad and the landing page wastes expensive compute cycles. Mismatches lead to lower delivery priority and increased CPMs.

##### Landing Page Routing Matrix

To maintain signal continuity, map your Visual Sub-Type to the following architectures:

1. **Grounded Sub-Type**  \-\>  **"Lightning Lander"** : High-speed, data-heavy, analytical pages featuring technical specs and tables.  
2. **Esoteric Sub-Type**  \-\>  **"Interactive Quiz"** : Focused on psychographic engagement and mindful, step-by-step engagement Affectgroup.  
3. **Aspirational Sub-Type**  \-\>  **"Coastal VSL"** : High-production Video Sales Letters and lifestyle-driven imagery.

##### Compliance Guardrails

To bypass AI policy blocks and ensure account longevity, we mandate these strict non-negotiables:

* **Zero Personal Attributes:**  Do not imply knowledge of a user’s race, health condition, or financial status (e.g., avoid "Are you struggling with...?") LSEO.  
* **Substantiated Claims:**  Exaggerated product benefits without credible backing lead to immediate disapproval LSEO.  
* **Transparency Requirements:**  Privacy policies and terms must be visible and mirror the ad's message LSEO.

##### Signal Protection: CAPI and Signals Gateway

We distinguish between our two primary server-side layers to ensure all conversions are attributed back to the correct Entity ID:

* **Conversions API (CAPI):**  Essential for server-to-server event delivery, mirroring browser signals to protect against ad-blockers and iOS limitations Meta Developers.  
* **Signals Gateway:**  Provides a dedicated environment to manage and enhance incoming data streams before they reach Meta’s ingestion points, ensuring maximum signal quality for GEM to learn "what the user will do next" Meta Developers.*Human editorial review remains the essential final check for truthfulness and brand safety. While the algorithm finds the audience via creative signals, the strategist provides the integrity AdRiseLab.*
