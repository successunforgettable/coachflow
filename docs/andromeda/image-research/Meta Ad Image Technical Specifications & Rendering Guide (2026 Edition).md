#### 2\. Placement-by-Placement Aspect Ratio & Composition Architecture

##### 2.1 Feed Placements (4:5 Vertical vs 1:1 Square)

The 4:5 vertical ratio ( $1440 \\times 1800 \\text{ px}$ ) is the technically superior format, yielding a 20–25% increase in mobile viewport real estate compared to legacy 1:1 assets.Operators must apply the  **Matched-Pair Principle** : visual intent must achieve semantic alignment with the primary copy hook. Any discrepancy between visual assets and copy hooks degrades the multi-dimensional semantic embedding generated by Andromeda’s computer vision classifiers, directly causing retrieval degradation and increased bounce rates.

##### 2.2 Stories & Reels (9:16 Vertical)

Immersive vertical delivery requires a native  $1080 \\times 1920 \\text{ px}$  resolution. This format serves as the primary vessel for the high-bandwidth retrieval pathway across the Meta ecosystem.

##### 2.3 Carousel Cards (1:1 Mandatory)

A 1:1 square ratio ( $1440 \\times 1440 \\text{ px}$ ) is mandatory. Andromeda executes unpredictable letterboxing or aggressive cropping on 4:5 assets within carousels, which collapses the asset's Creative Similarity Score and penalizes the Entity ID.

##### 2.4 Threads Feed (1:1 and 4:5 Max)

Threads supports a maximum aspect ratio of 4:5. Operators are warned that 9:16 assets are  **center-cropped**  to 4:5 automatically. All primary visual action and typography must inhabit the  **Center Safe Band**  to mitigate terminal information loss during this transform.

#### 3\. Unified 2026 Safe-Zone Specifications & Composition Rules

##### 3.1 The March 2026 Unified Consolidation

As of March 2026, safe zones across Facebook and Instagram Stories and Reels have been synchronized to prevent UI collision.

##### 3.2 Precise Canvas Mapping ( $1080 \\times 1920$ )

To ensure visual integrity across the global hardware fleet, maintain the following clearance requirements:

* **Top 14% (\~250px):**  UI Header/Profile clearance.  
* **Bottom 20–35% (\~340–670px):**  CTA Button and Caption/Overlay clearance.  
* **Side 6% (\~65px):**  Variance buffer for edge-to-edge rendering.

##### 3.3 The Center Safe Band

The  **Center Safe Band**  constitutes the middle 51% of the vertical canvas (pixels  **250 to 1248** ). All critical visual elements (logos, faces, primary headlines) must be positioned within this coordinate range to guarantee visibility across all transform layers.

#### 4\. Resolution, Encoding & Asset File Hygiene

##### 4.1 High-Density Rendering Standards

The benchmark for professional rendering is  **1440p**  ( $1440 \\times 1800$  for Feed). This higher density is required to mitigate automated compression artifacts during the Stage 1 retrieval phase.

##### 4.2 Static Image Hygiene (JPG vs PNG)

* **Color Profile:**  sRGB mandatory.  
* **Metadata:**  Strip all PNG metadata to reduce latent file weight.  
* **Retrieval Target:**  Maintain \<5MB for high-speed Andromeda ingestion.

##### 4.3 Video Encoding Infrastructure

* **Containers:**  MP4 or MOV.  
* **Codec:**  H.264 is  **mandatory** . H.265/HEVC assets will be rejected by the ingestion engine.  
* **Audio:**  AAC at 128kbps+.  
* **Frame Rate:**  23.976 to 30 fps.

##### 4.4 In-Image Typography & Density

The "20% Rule" is retired. Assets must now adhere to the  **\<33% Canvas Coverage**  rule.

* **Headline Limit:**  5–7 words maximum.  
* **Retrieval Penalty:**  Density exceeding 33% triggers visual complexity penalties within the Andromeda retrieval engine, resulting in sub-optimal ranking.

#### 5\. 2026 Platform Updates & Algorithmic Considerations

##### 5.1 Traffic Migration

Following the retirement of the Instagram Explore Feed, traffic has been migrated to Reels and the Main Feed. Asset optimization must focus on these vertical high-density environments.

##### 5.2 Placement Asset Customization (PAC) & Entity IDs

Native assets for every ratio (1:1, 4:5, 9:16) trigger distinct  **Entity IDs** . Reliance on auto-letterboxing results in  **Creative Similarity Scores**  exceeding 60%, leading to retrieval suppression. To achieve a Similarity Score \<40% and unlock new Entity IDs, operators must move beyond "Cosmetic Variation" (hex code swaps) and implement "Structural Variation" across four pillars:

1. **Environmental Lighting/Palettes:**  Shifting from high-key studio light to warm, residential natural light.  
2. **Talent/Human Inclusion:**  Varying faces, demographics, and body language.  
3. **Physical Backdrops:**  Shifting from minimalist offices to outdoor landscapes or residential scenes.  
4. **Compositional Format:**  Alternating between portraits, diagrams, and carousels.

##### 5.3 Multimodal Compliance (MARS)

The Multimodal Ad Review System (MARS) scans for the following prohibited patterns:

* **Pronoun Traps:**  Pairing "you/your" with protected attributes (health, finance, spiritual).  
* **Implied Transformations:**  Prohibited before/after sequences, specifically placing a product next to an  **idealized silhouette**  or featuring  **unlabeled side-by-side photo sequences** .

##### 5.4 Pre-Launch Technical Verification Checklist

Before graduating assets to scaling campaigns, verify the following:

1. **Resolution:**  1440p (Feed) or 1080p (Stories/Reels).  
2. **Safe-Zone Compliance:**  Critical elements within pixels 250–1248.  
3. **Text Density:**  \<33% coverage; 5–7 word max headline.  
4. **Entity ID Uniqueness:**  Concepts must be structurally diverse (Similarity Score \<40%).  
5. **Codec Compatibility:**  H.264/AAC in MP4/MOV container.
