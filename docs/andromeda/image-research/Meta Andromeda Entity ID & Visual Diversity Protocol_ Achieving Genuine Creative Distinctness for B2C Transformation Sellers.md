### Meta Andromeda Entity ID & Visual Diversity Protocol: Achieving Genuine Creative Distinctness for B2C Transformation Sellers

#### 1\. Executive Blueprint: The Andromeda Retrieval Engine and the Entity ID Mechanism

##### The Technical Shift: Retrieval-Led Delivery

In the 2026 Meta advertising landscape, the core mechanism of delivery has transitioned from manual audience segmentation to creative-led retrieval. The  **Andromeda Retrieval Engine**  serves as the "Stage 1" gateway, a computational bouncer that filters millions of potential ads into a shortlist of approximately 1,000 candidates before they ever reach the  **Stage 2 Ranking**  layer (Lattice/GEM).While Andromeda handles the retrieval of current intent based on semantic embeddings, the  **GEM (Generative Ads Recommendation Model)**  layer utilizes sequence modelling to predict future intent. By analysing chains of user behaviour, GEM determines "what the user will do next" rather than just responding to what they are doing now. To pass this gauntlet, an ad must first be retrieved by Andromeda; if it fails this initial vector-distance calculation, the ad effectively ceases to exist within the auction.

##### Technical Infrastructure: Hardware-Accelerated Inference

Andromeda’s ability to process these decisions in microseconds is powered by a high-performance hardware stack:

* **NVIDIA Grace Hopper Superchip & MTIA v2:**  This bespoke silicon eliminates traditional memory bandwidth bottlenecks, allowing for a 10,000x expansion in model capacity.  
* **Hierarchical Index Tree:**  Andromeda organizes eligible ads into a multi-layered index tree. Using sublinear inference, the model navigates from "root" to specific "branches" (e.g., Wellness \> Somatic Practice \> Anxiety Relief), pruning irrelevant assets to maintain efficiency.

##### The Logic of Entity IDs and Vector Clustering

When an asset is uploaded, computer vision classifiers generate a  **semantic embedding** —a multi-dimensional mathematical fingerprint representing its visual DNA.

* **The Entity ID Mechanism:**  Multiple Creative IDs (individual uploads) are grouped under a single internal  **Entity ID**  if they exhibit significant  **Coordinate Overlap** .  
* **The Branch-Cutting Rule:**  The Andromeda gateway executes a vector-distance calculation to determine auction eligibility.  
* **Similarity \>60%:**  Triggers  **Vector Clustering** . The system collapses these ads into a single Entity ID, granting only one auction "ticket." This results in auction suppression and inflated account-wide CPMs.  
* **Similarity \<40%:**  Unlocks a unique Entity ID. This allows the asset to explore independent branches of the index tree, accessing distinct audience segments the original asset could not reach.

#### 2\. Structural vs Cosmetic Visual Variables

To achieve scale in the B2C transformation sector, creative teams must stop iterating on variables that cause Entity ID collapse and focus on variables that unlock new branches of the index tree.

##### Structural Analysis (Unlocks New Entity IDs)

* **Talent & Demographic Inclusion:**  Changing the featured face, age, or body type immediately breaks the semantic grouping.  
* **Environmental Coordinates:**  Moving from a sterile clinical studio to a natural outdoor landscape or a warm residential setting provides unique spatial signals.  
* **Visual Composition & Format:**  Alternating between a photographic portrait, a hand-drawn "Method-Made-Visible" whiteboard diagram, or a minimalist text-heavy card forces a distinct mathematical layout.  
* **Lighting & Emotional Register:**  Transitioning from high-key, bright daylight to low-key, "atmospheric" lighting with deep shadows signals a new visual context to the classifier.

##### Cosmetic Analysis (Causes Entity ID Collapse)

* **Copy & Overlay Swaps:**  Changing headlines or hex codes on the same background template.  
* **Minor Framing:**  Slight zooms, crops, or padding adjustments on the same person/environment.  
* **Accessory Toggling:**  Changing a shirt colour or a minor prop on the same talent in the same room.

#### 4\. Sub-Type Execution across the B2C Transformation Spectrum

##### Clinical & Grounded Practitioners (Therapists, Nutritionists)

* **Strategy:**  Build trust via clinical authority and evidence-based safety.  
* **Execution:**  Use soft, even lighting. Deploy "Method-Made-Visible" flowcharts to translate abstract concepts into logical pathways.

##### Aspirational & Lifestyle Coaches (Mindset, Relationship)

* **Strategy:**  Generate desire through hope and future-state identity shifts.  
* **Execution:**  Use "moment" shots with high-contrast, natural lighting. Showcase the emotional outcome in bright, open spaces.

##### Esoteric & Spiritual Practitioners (Tarot, Energy Healers)

* **Strategy:**  Maintain credibility through atmospheric and tactile imagery.  
* **Execution:**  Use low-key lighting and deep shadows. Focus on symbolic textures (raw stone, linen) to avoid a commercial aesthetic that triggers  **scepticism** .

##### Compliance Guardrails (MARS & Community Framing)

The Multimodal Ad Review System (MARS) proactively screens for "pronoun traps" and "implied transformations."

* **The Community Framing Solution:**  To bypass the "pronoun trap," move from personal attributes to shared experiences. Instead of "Your anxiety," use "The anxious nervous system."

#### 5\. Algorithmic Deployment & Creative Refresh Cadence

##### Testing Infrastructure: The ABO Protocol

To isolate winning Entity IDs without algorithmic bias, we mandate an  **ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimisation)**  structure:

* **Structure:**  One ad per ad set.  
* **Targeting:**  Completely broad (no interests, no lookalikes). Let the creative embedding handle retrieval.  
* **Budget:**  Equal allocation across all 8–12 assets to ensure clean data collection.

##### Leading Indicator Monitoring

Evaluate performance via these metrics before CPA stabilises:

* **Outbound Click Ratio (OCR):**  Goal  **\>70%** . Ratios below this indicate a visual-to-copy mismatch.  
* **Qualified Visitor Cost (QVC):**  Spend / (Link Clicks × Landing Page View Rate).  
* **Creative Efficiency Ratio (CER):**  (Conversions / Impressions) × 1,000.
