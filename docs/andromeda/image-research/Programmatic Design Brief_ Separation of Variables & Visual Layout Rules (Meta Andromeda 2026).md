### Meta Andromeda Programmatic Design Brief & Brand Standards (2026)

#### 1\. Operational Blueprint: The ‘Separation of Variables’ Principle

In the 2026 Meta Andromeda ecosystem, the manual targeting era has been superseded by a regime of creative-led retrieval. Andromeda acts as a high-velocity "bouncer" at the retrieval stage, leveraging a hardware-enforced bottleneck powered by the  **NVIDIA Grace Hopper Superchip and MTIA v2**  architecture. This custom silicon allows for a 10,000x increase in model complexity, processing millions of candidate ads in microseconds to determine audience fit before an ad even enters the bidding auction. Andromeda’s computer vision classifiers generate a multi-dimensional  **semantic embedding**  for every asset; if the creative DNA fails this Stage 1 filter, it is mathematically excluded from delivery.To maintain delivery efficiency, we must navigate the mechanism of  **Entity ID clustering** . Andromeda groups semantically similar ads under a single internal fingerprint. Our programmatic objective is to maintain a  **Creative Similarity Score below 40%** . Scores exceeding 60% lead to retrieval suppression, as clustered ads share a single "retrieval ticket," causing inflated account-wide CPMs. To unlock independent auction pathways, we must execute structural—not cosmetic—variation.

#### 2\. Programmatic Design Templates: Visual Layout & Technical Specifications

In the 2026 B2C transformation market, credibility requires aligning visual archetypes with specific expert sub-types. This alignment ensures the Andromeda retrieval engine correctly categorizes the ad for the appropriate psychological cohort.

##### 2.1. Grounded & Professional (Clinical Template)

Designed for practitioners (Therapists, Nutritionists, Advisors) requiring high-authority, evidence-based positioning.

* **Target Awareness:**   **Solution-Aware** . This cohort craves the logical structure provided by the "Method Made Visible."  
* **Grid Structure:**  Centered focal coordinate targets with strict negative space boundaries to accommodate labeled diagrams.  
* **Lighting Specs:**  Soft, even, and diffused lighting at a colour temperature of  **5500K**  (Neutral/Clinical).  
* **Typography:**  High-contrast sans-serif pairings (e.g., Inter, Montserrat).  
* **Visual Logic:**  Emphasize IP via labeled system models or anatomical outlines.**The "So What?" Layer:**  This aesthetic establishes "Safety and Evidence-Based Support" hooks. It provides the structured mechanism required to convert prospects who are aware of the solution but haven't committed to a specific methodology.

##### 2.2. Spiritual & Esoteric (Tactile/Atmospheric Template)

Designed for spiritual practitioners where high-key studio lighting destroys niche credibility.

* **Grid Structure:**  Rule of thirds with off-centre focal targets and hand-motion placements.  
* **Lighting Specs:**  Low-key, warm candlelight with deep directional shadows at  **2700K** .  
* **Backdrop:**  Organic textures (e.g., dark linen, raw stone, worn wood).  
* **Typography:**  Elegant serif or expressive hand-written script pairings.**The "So What?" Layer:**  In this niche, "Atmospheric Reflection" validates the consumer's emotional state. Deviating from this atmospheric rigour signals commercial insincerity to the prospect.

##### 2.3. Aspirational & Lifestyle (Vitality/Freedom Template)

Designed for mindset or fitness coaching, focusing on future identity shifts.

* **Target Awareness:**   **Unaware** . The lo-fi, organic feed aesthetic serves as the required "Pattern Break" for those oblivious to their friction.  
* **Grid Structure:**  Open grid with expansive environmental boundaries and active mid-motion compositions.  
* **Lighting Specs:**  High-key natural daylight at  **4500K–5000K** .  
* **Typography:**  Clean modern sans-serif or humanist serifs.  
* **Visual Logic:**  Candid "moment" shots that mimic native platform content.**The "So What?" Layer:**  By generating desire through "Future State" imagery, this template bypasses defensive buying psychology and prevents delivery volume decline by aligning with platform-native visual signals.

Under the 2026 Multimodal Ad Review System (MARS), UI clearance is a functional delivery requirement. MARS evaluates ads across five data layers; if platform UI obscures content, engagement signals are degraded, and delivery priority drops.

#### 4\. Interactive Step-by-Step QA Verification Checklist

This serves as the final "operator-grade" filter before asset graduation to Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO).

1. **Variable Separation:**  Verify that 'Variables of Change' (Environment, Talent, or Composition) are structurally distinct from previous Entity IDs to maintain a Similarity Score \< 40%.  
2. **Awareness Alignment:**  Confirm the visual mechanism matches the Schwartz Awareness Stage (e.g., Labeled diagrams for  **Solution-Aware** ; Lo-fi lifestyle for  **Unaware** ).  
3. **Technical Temperature:**  Check that the Kelvin lighting profile matches the sub-type requirement (Clinical: 5500K, Esoteric: 2700K, Aspirational: 4500K).  
4. **UI Clearance:**  Validate zero content enters the 250px top or 670px bottom UI zones; all focal elements must reside in the Center Safe Band.  
5. **Policy Guardrails:**  Bypass the  **"Pronoun Trap"**  by ensuring no combination of "You/Your" with protected attributes (health, finance, beliefs). Use "Community Framing" to avoid MARS rejection triggers.**Adherence to this brief transforms the creative from a static asset into a high-functioning targeting signal for the Andromeda ecosystem.**
