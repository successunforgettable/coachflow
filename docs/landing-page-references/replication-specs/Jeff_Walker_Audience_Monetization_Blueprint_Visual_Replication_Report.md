## 21. Highest-risk section

### Selected highest-risk section: hero → large blue continuation → white “CONGRATULATIONS!” interruption

This is the highest-risk section because its fidelity depends on an unconventional vertical relationship, not component styling alone.

- **Generic implementation failure:** build a normal viewport-height hero and place the white card immediately below it, eliminating the desktop’s signature blue suspension.
