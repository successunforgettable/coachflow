## 23. Highest-risk section

### Selected highest-risk section: the “150+ Experts Went from 0 to 1 Cr+” 12-card portrait/result grid

This is the most difficult single section because its fidelity depends on a large number of repeated but content-sensitive elements. Every portrait has different source framing, while the card system must remain optically uniform. Names, roles, and result badges vary in length. The white offer bar intersects the region in the frozen evidence, adding a potentially obscuring layer whose mechanics cannot be assumed.

- **Desktop risk:** incorrect 3-column or 6-column choice destroys the 4×3 rhythm; inconsistent image crops and text heights produce ragged rows.
- **Mobile risk:** one-column stacking makes the section much too long; three columns make faces/text unreadable. The frozen signature is 2×6.
- **Image/copy risk:** arbitrary headshots with too much torso or background will make faces appear tiny.
- **Section-order risk:** placing this before the Hall of Fame image reverses the frozen authority progression.
- **Proof-role risk:** relabelling all cards as testimonials or speakers invents roles not established by the visual.
