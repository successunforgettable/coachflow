### Policy Analysis: Meta Personal Attributes and Privacy Violations Standards

##### 1\. Official Policy Definition and Core Mandate

The Personal Attributes policy constitutes a fundamental pillar of Meta’s Advertising Standards. It prohibits advertisers from running content that asserts or implies personal details about the viewer. The core mandate of this policy is to maintain a high-quality user experience by preventing advertisements from feeling invasive or creating an uncomfortable "feeling of surveillance."Advertisers must recognize that the platform is designed to protect users from the perception that their sensitive, private information is being utilized for commercial targeting. Even if the backend targeting of an ad is technically accurate, the creative and copy must never confirm to the viewer that Meta possesses or is acting upon specific knowledge of their private life. The standard is simple: ad copy must never allow a user to feel "watched."

##### 2\. Comprehensive Inventory of Prohibited Personal Attributes

Meta identifies a broad spectrum of protected characteristics that cannot be addressed directly or implied through ad content. The following attributes are strictly protected:

1. **Race or Ethnic Origin:**  Any reference to skin color, ancestry, or ethnic background.  
2. **Religion or Philosophical Beliefs:**  Assertions regarding faith, religious affiliation, or philosophical convictions.  
3. **Age and Senior Status:**  Direct references to a person’s specific age or status as a "senior."  
4. **Sexual Orientation or Gender Identity:**  Any mention of sexual preferences, gender status, or LGBTQ+ identity.  
5. **Disability or Medical/Health Conditions:**  References to physical or mental impairments, specific diagnoses, or symptoms.  
6. **Financial Status:**  Content referencing debt, bankruptcy, credit scores, or specific income levels.  
7. **Voting History or Political Affiliation:**  Assertions regarding a person’s voting record or political party membership.  
8. **Criminal Record or Legal History:**  References to past criminal activity or current immigration status.  
9. **Membership in Sensitive Groups:**  References to specific affiliations, such as trade unions.

##### 3\. The Mechanics of Violation: Identification of Triggers

Advertisers must acknowledge that Meta’s automated review systems utilize sophisticated markers to identify breaches. These triggers function through three technical mechanisms:**The Second-Person Address (The "You" Trap)**  The primary trigger for rejection is the use of the second-person pronouns "you" or "your" in immediate proximity to a protected attribute. By addressing the viewer directly, the ad asserts that the platform has identified the viewer as possessing that trait. For example, asking "Are you over 60?" or stating "Your debt is rising" acknowledges the viewer’s personal circumstances and causes an immediate violation.**Semantic Intent and Loophole Closure**  Meta utilizes semantic intent detection to flag indirect references that suggest knowledge of a viewer's private life. This mechanism serves as a "loophole closure," effectively equating specific symptoms with a diagnosis. For instance, mentioning "blood sugar spikes" or "glucose levels" in a "you" context is treated as an assertion of a diabetes diagnosis. If the context suggests the advertiser is addressing a sensitive personal state, the ad will be rejected regardless of whether the specific attribute is named.**The Assertion of Knowledge vs. Implication**  Meta maintains a strict dichotomy between "assertion" (direct statement) and "implication" (indirect suggestion). A violation occurs whenever the copy confirms Meta’s targeting precision to the user. While interest-based targeting is permitted in the backend, the creative must remain agnostic to that targeting to avoid triggering the user's feeling of being monitored.

##### 5\. Category-Specific Restrictions: Health, Finance, and Web3

Certain sensitive categories face additional scrutiny where Personal Attributes intersect with broader safety standards:

* **Health and Wellness:**  
* **Negative Self-Perception:**  Content must not generate shame or focus on "perfect" body types.  
* **Transformation Imagery:**  Side-by-side "before and after" photos are strictly prohibited for weight loss and anti-aging/wrinkle treatments, as they imply guaranteed, unrealistic outcomes.  
* **Financial and Income Claims:**  
* **Prohibited Guarantees:**  Phrases such as "Earn $500/day" or "Replace your salary in 30 days" are banned. Claims must describe realistic mechanisms, not guaranteed dollar amounts.  
* **Cryptocurrency and Web3:**  
* **Permitted:**  Ads for events, news, education, and hardware wallets designed solely for holding private keys without trading services.  
* **Restricted:**  Software wallets with swapping/staking features and crypto exchanges require prior written permission and regulatory licensing.  
* **EU Compliance:**  Cryptocurrency ads in the EU must adhere to MiCA Article 29 disclosure requirements.  
* **Political Advertising:**  
* **EU Restrictions:**  Advertisers must note that as of October 2025, political, electoral, and social issue ads are undeliverable in the EU.

##### 6\. Boundaries and Undefined Gray Zones

* **General Demographics:**  Referencing broad, non-sensitive demographics is acceptable. "Attention New Yorkers" or "For Americans" is permitted as these are geographic identifiers, not sensitive attributes.  
* **Aspirational Pivoting:**  Compliance is achieved by shifting from identity-based language to "interest-based" language. Describing an interest in a topic is permitted; asserting that the user  *is*  a certain type of person is not.  
* **Mechanism vs. Outcome:**  Detailing a "realistic mechanism" (how a product works) is allowed. However, any claim that guarantees an "outcome" typically triggers rejections for being misleading.

##### 7\. Strategic Compliance: The Three-Step Reframing Protocol

To successfully pass review, advertisers should implement this proprietary methodology:**Step 1: De-personalization (Third-Person Shift)**  Remove all instances of "you" and "your." Replace them with "People who..." or "Individuals managing..." to neutralize the address and move the focus away from the specific viewer.**Step 2: Aspirational Pivoting (Benefit Focus)**  Highlight the solution or the benefit rather than the diagnosis. On a technical level, use  **Neutralized Custom Events**  (e.g., tracking a conversion as Goal\_Complete instead of Purchase or Lead) to align with 2025-2026 data privacy restrictions for sensitive categories.**Step 3: Neutralization of Language**  Use broad, inclusive language that emphasizes community or innovation rather than individual traits. Frame the product as an available resource for a general interest group rather than a targeted treatment for an individual.

##### 8\. Final Compliance Audit Summary

Before submission, advertisers must confirm the following:

*   **Pronoun Check:**  Does the copy avoid "you" or "your" in proximity to sensitive traits?  
*   **Benefit Alignment:**  Is the focus on the product mechanism rather than a viewer’s condition?  
*   **Imagery Audit:**  Have side-by-side transformation photos been removed for health/beauty?  
*   **Claim Verification:**  Are income claims realistic and free of dollar-amount guarantees?  
*   **Landing Page Alignment:**  Does the landing page exactly match the ad's claims (e.g., if "Free Shipping" is in the ad, it is not gated by a minimum purchase on the site)?  
*   **Regional Authorization:**  For Web3 or Finance, has written permission been obtained and have MiCA/EU-specific requirements been met?
