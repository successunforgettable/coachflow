# Meta compliance — the SOURCE reports (banked 2026-08-03)

The fifteen research reports that `../META_AD_COMPLIANCE_REFERENCE.md` was synthesised from. That
reference's own header cites "a research sweep (~15 reports)" — these are those reports. Until today
the synthesis was in version control and its sources were **only in `~/Downloads`**.

**⚠️ THE SYNTHESIS OUTRANKS THESE FILES. Read `../META_AD_COMPLIANCE_REFERENCE.md` first, always.**

That is not a formality. The reference exists precisely *because* this raw material could not be
trusted wholesale: a large part of the sweep returned claims that sounded authoritative but appear
**nowhere in Meta's published documentation** — several reports filed them under headings like
"Verified Policy Realities". The reference's tier split (Tier 1 confirmed policy · Tier 2
practitioner-reported · Tier 3) and its **DO-NOT-BUILD list** are the product of separating the two.

**So: never promote a claim from these files into enforcement logic without checking it against the
reference's tiering first.** Only Tier 1 may become a hard gate. These are banked for provenance and
for re-checking the synthesis — not as a build source.
