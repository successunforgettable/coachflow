My Program

I'm a fitness coach and I help busy women in their 40s lose weight and feel strong again without spending hours in the gym. A lot of my clients are mums who used to be fit but life got in the way - work, kids, no time for themselves. They feel tired all the time and don't recognise their body anymore.

My program is called Strong Again. It's 12 weeks, fully online. We do short workouts you can do at home (20-30 mins), a simple meal plan that isn't a crazy diet, and weekly check-ins with me so you stay on track. I also have a WhatsApp group for the women so they support each other.

It costs £1,500 for the 12 weeks.

The main thing that makes me different is I don't do the punishing bootcamp thing. It's realistic for busy people. Most of my clients have tried other diets and programs before and quit because it was too much. With me they actually stick to it because it fits their life.

I've helped over 200 women so far. A lot of them tell me they finally have energy again and feel confident in their clothes.
